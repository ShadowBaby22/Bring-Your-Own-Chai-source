# Playtest: round 3, juror

**Persona:** industry juror (12 years of mobile and casual design at studios like Zynga and Miniclip). This is one of 200 entries tonight. I judge the first 60 seconds hard and try to reach a boss.
**Build:** `http://localhost:8765/` (dev build Sun 4 Oct 02:14), 450x800 viewport.
**Furthest:** Day 4. I beat the first boss (The Broker) and stopped at the Day 4 hub (Lv 4, ₹660).
**Time:** about 13.5 min wall clock, roughly 6 min of real play. About 88 actions, over the 60-action budget (see the caveat).

> **Test-environment caveat.** The browser pane was hidden for the whole session (`tabs_context` reported it), so Unity ran throttled. Because of that:
> - the first click after nearly every screen change was dropped;
> - fights ran far slower than real time;
> - Baba's 10-second timer and the brew meter sometimes ran out before my tap arrived.
>
> I did not count any of this against the game. I make no judgement of fight pacing or meter difficulty. Sound FX and Music were both OFF from a saved setting, so **audio is not assessed**.

## Play log (play time, not wall clock)
- **0:00** Title screen: KADAK Chai logo, two characters and a NO BACHELORS sign. Tagline: "Bablu. 11 days. One stamp. No Bachelors. Everything is temporary." I knew the goal and the theme before my first tap. I checked the gear: Sound FX OFF, Music OFF. Then I tapped Sign the Agreement.
- **0:20** The intro is five short lines with typewriter text and a Bablu portrait. "Population: yes. Air quality: also yes." got a smile. The phone hub then sums up the premise in one message, including the Amma's Nursing Home safety net.
- **0:50** Meet the Astrologer opens Baba's blessing: three cards, each with a "Lease: N days" line, a "Baba leaves in 10" bar, and the warning "Some are fake." I picked Pressure Horn, and a "Horn 3d" chip appeared.
- **1:10** In JhatPat, the free Mystery Box gave a RARE Badminton Racket with a reveal burst, and I equipped it. Bablu now visibly holds the racket. The shop lists today's enemies and their range types, and each weapon shows "Lasts N fights".
- **1:40** **Day 1** fight against South Delhi Girl, whose HP bar is "Followers 60/60". The brew meter appeared: "Brew it KADAK! Tap anywhere when the glass hits the dark middle", with labelled zones pheeka / theek / KADAK / theek / pheeka. I got Pheeka, and the log said "Pheeki chai. No boost this fight." Big yellow damage starbursts and confetti followed, and I won in 2 rounds. Level up to Lv 2.
- **2:20** Baba again. I took "Do You Know Who My Father Is?" and it fizzled: "Stars are cloudy today. No refunds." I had been warned, and it still made me grin.
- **2:40** **Day 2** event. Pinky Aunty, the RWA WhatsApp admin, has a two-portrait dialogue: "Engineer?! Beta, my WiFi is not working since Diwali…" and "You are like my son. Who is in Canada. And never calls." I used Skip, then chose "Leave her on read" over "Forward it". HP dropped from 121 to 108 with no visible reason. Only after I scrolled the Messages panel did I see "She joins anyway… Pinky Aunty joins you! -13 HP". She now walks behind Bablu as a companion.
- **3:20** **Day 3**: a new background (the inside of a Rajiv Chowk metro coach) with enemies Jaanu and Shona ("Love 60/60" and "Love 50/50"). GRAP 1 gives enemies +10% ATK. The brew came out Pheeka again. I won and reached Lv 3. I picked Anti-Smog Gun, and a big green **BLESSED** stamp slammed on. In the Map app, the button turned red: **Next Day: BOSS >**.
- **4:10** **Day 4 boss: The Broker** ("Deposit 190/190") gets a VS splash screen. The log read "Pressure Horn was only temporary. Like everything here." The brew result: "Chai got cold." The fight was a close HP race (both sides near 59 at one point), and the boss heals with barks like "Owner is very nice +13". I won. The Broker haggles himself down from half brokerage to a quarter, then throws in a free "temporary address proof… Valid for 11 months, sir. Like everything." My racket broke after the fight.
- **5:30** Post-boss, Baba offered Jugaad Shield, N95 Mask and Forward This Message. My tap was dropped (environment), and "Astrologer Baba got bored and left… No blessing." I stopped at the Day 4 hub.

**Audience score: 7/10.** Keep playing past 10 minutes: **no**. I wanted the next joke more than the next fight. Recommend to a friend: **yes**, especially anyone who has hunted for a flat in NCR.

## Top 3 frictions
1. **Low agency once Fight! is pressed.** After one tap on the brew meter, you watch. A miss only shows "no boost this fight", and I couldn't feel the meter change anything. Day 1 was over in 2 rounds. The meter itself is clear (labelled zones, big result words like "Pheeka…" and "Chai got cold."), but before the tap it never says what KADAK actually gives (+X% ATK? a crit?). Put the payoff on the meter.
2. **Results and warnings get lost in the Messages log.** The Day 2 choice cost 13 HP and added a companion, but the panel still showed the prompt; I only found the outcome by scrolling. The "gear up at JhatPat now: there is no shop between you and the boss" warning also sat in the log where I missed it, so I walked into the boss at 93/144 HP without shopping. Big outcomes need a toast or a stamp the way BLESSED gets one. Event choices could also hint at their stakes.
3. **Unexplained labels.** A "Cancelled" status chip sat on Bablu from Day 1 to Day 4, and I never learned what it does. The speed button flips between "2x" and "0.5x", and it isn't clear whether it shows the current speed or the next one.

## Top 3 delights
1. **The writing.** The intro gag about population and air quality, Pinky Aunty's son in Canada who never calls, the "No refunds" fizzle, and the Broker bargaining himself down to a free 11-month address proof. Each line is short, specific and lands.
2. **The theme as a system.** "Lease: 3 days" on every card, "Lasts 3 fights" on every weapon, chips that tick down, and the line "Pressure Horn was only temporary. Like everything here." This is the best use of the theme I'd expect to see tonight.
3. **Readable and juicy UI.** The first-60-seconds clarity is excellent: tagline, one-message premise, and a big button that always says what's next (Meet the Astrologer, Start Day 1, Fight!, Next Day: BOSS). Renamed HP bars, the VS splash, the BLESSED stamp, the Rare reveal and enemy barks all make an auto-battler feel alive.

## Bugs
- **Possible: the Messages log doesn't scroll to the event outcome.** Steps: Day 2, Pinky Aunty event, choose "Leave her on read", then watch the Messages panel without scrolling. HP drops (121 to 108), but the panel still shows the prompt. "Pinky Aunty joins you! -13 HP" only appeared after I scrolled down. This may be a late-arriving message rather than a scroll bug; the hidden-pane throttle could be involved.
- **Not game bugs (environment):** dropped first clicks, slow fights, the Baba timer expiring before my tap, and the brew meter reading "Chai got cold" before my tap registered. All are explained by the hidden, throttled pane.
- No console errors.
