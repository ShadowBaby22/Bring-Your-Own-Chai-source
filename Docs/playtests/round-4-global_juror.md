# Round 4 playtest: global_juror

**Persona:** jury member from outside India. I don't know Hinglish, NCR, RWAs, GRAP or Indian bureaucracy. I'm judging this against about 200 jam entries tonight.
**Build:** `http://localhost:8765/` ("dev build Sun 4 Oct 02:34"), 450x800 viewport, music and SFX already OFF (I checked the gear once).
**Played:** about 6 minutes, 38 actions. Reached the end of Day 3 (fight won, boss warning shown) and stopped there as briefed.

## Play log

- **0:00 Title.** "Bablu. 11 days. One stamp. No Bachelors. *Everything is temporary.*" The art is clean and the NO BACHELORS sign tells me the conflict straight away. The line at the bottom ("Agla station: Temporary. Darwaze baayi taraf khulenge.") looks like the theme punchline, but it's in Hindi and I can't read it. I don't know what "KADAK" means. Tapped Sign the Agreement.
- **0:45 Intro.** Bablu: 21, B.Tech, "four backlogs", a Gurugram internship, accommodation "figure it out". "Delhi NCR. Population: yes. Air quality: also yes." made me laugh. To rent a flat a bachelor needs "police verification, stamped at the Sarkari Daftar", and he has 11 days. A recap card in the phone's Messages app sums it up well, but it says "The RWAs want police verification" and never explains RWA.
- **1:45 Astrologer Baba.** "Pick fast. Blessings are temporary. Some are fake." The three cards each have a "Lease: N days" tag, which made the theme click for me. I picked Jugaad Shield; the effect text explained it, though the word "Jugaad" didn't. A note says JhatPat is installed, with deliveries "in 10 minutes (T&C apply)", so I got the delivery-app parody. I opened JhatPat and claimed the free Mystery Box, which gave me **Health Insurance** (rare). Its icon is a blank white circle. I tapped "Pop it" without knowing whether that meant keep it or destroy it, then pressed "Back to the lease" (back to what?). The chat then confirmed "Insured 3d", which cleared it up.
- **3:00 Day 1 fight.** South Delhi Girl, HP shown as **Followers 60/60**, saying "Like, literally, can you move? This is content." The stereotype is lost on me but the influencer joke lands fully. Tapped Fight!. The "Brew it KADAK!" meter appeared and is easy to read: "Tap when the glass hits the dark middle. KADAK = +40% ATK for 3 rounds". My tap arrived late: "The chai got cold. No boost this fight." I won in about 3 rounds at 2x speed. Baba came back straight away. By my first look the bar said "Baba leaves in 5", and my tap missed: "Baba got bored and left." Level 2, ₹280.
- **4:15 Day 2 event.** Pinky Aunty, "RWA WhatsApp admin. I wrote that board myself." Then "Engineer?! Beta, my WiFi is not working since Diwali", Bablu "(turns it off and on again)", "You are like my son. Who is in Canada. And never calls." This is the best writing in the game, and it works in any country. I chose "Leave her on read". She "joins anyway, and tells the whole society you are 'very rude'", and she now follows me as a companion (-13 HP). Lovely.
- **5:30 Day 3.** Metro, "Rajiv Chowk", a PDA couple named Jaanu and Shona (HP is **Love 60/60**, and he wears a HER JAANU shirt) blocking the only door. Read two lines, then used Skip >>. A line explains the HUD a little: "AQI 412. GRAP 3, Construction Ban: skills trigger half as often." Fight!, then the meter, then "Pheeki chai. No boost this fight. Tap when the glass is in the dark middle." Won in 5 rounds (DHISHOOM!). Baba again showed 5 seconds left when I first saw him, and I missed again. Now Lv 3, ₹360, and "Tomorrow: BOSS FIGHT with The Broker. Heal up and gear up at JhatPat now." That warning is clear. **Stopped here.**

The air quality number climbs every day (AQI 260, then 325, then 412), and the GRAP number goes 1, 2, 3 alongside it. I worked out that smog gets worse and something gets banned. It's a good mechanic, but the HUD never names it.

## Words/jokes that need a gloss

Suggested glosses keep the original word and add a few English words. Players learn the flavour words; they don't lose them.

| Where | Word / line | Suggested gloss |
|---|---|---|
| Title footer | "Agla station: Temporary. Darwaze baayi taraf khulenge." | Add a small second line: *"Next station: Temporary. Doors open on the left."* It's the best theme joke on the screen. |
| Title / meter | KADAK | Title subtitle *"(strong) chai"*, or on the meter *"Brew it KADAK! (extra strong)"* |
| Brew meter | pheeka / theek / KADAK | *pheeka (weak) · theek (okay) · KADAK (strong)*. Also make the label "pheeka" and the result "Pheeki chai" use the same word. |

## Top 3 frictions
1. **The top HUD and the title are all jargon or untranslated.** KHATRA, AQI/GRAP chips, KADAK and the Hindi tagline. The first screen I see contains the one joke I can't read. A world juror forms a first impression in 60 seconds, and some of that time goes on "what is GRAP?".
2. **Baba's 10-second pick starts the moment the fight ends.** There's no breather or win beat in between. Both times I first saw him he was at "Baba leaves in 5", and I lost both picks. My browser lag is part of that, so I'm not blaming the game for the misses, but a non-native reader needs time to parse three cards with words like "Jugaad" and "Pressure Horn". Suggest starting the countdown after the cards finish animating in, or giving 15 seconds on the first visit.
3. **Shop and box copy and art are unclear.** "Pop it" vs "Return", "Back to the lease", and Health Insurance with a blank icon made the one shop visit feel unfinished. The "Insured 3d" chip afterwards was clear.

## Top 3 delights
1. **The writing travels.** Short, dry, universal lines: "Population: yes. Air quality: also yes."; Pinky Aunty's WiFi and Canada son; boats in Gurgaon. Best-written jokes I've seen in a jam this year.
2. **The theme is mechanical and visible.** "Lease: N days" on every blessing, expiring insurance, breakable weapons, smog that worsens daily and bans things. It's the clearest "Everything Is Temporary" reading I've seen tonight.
3. **HP bars as jokes.** Followers 60/60, Love 60/60, the HER JAANU shirt, and an event where ignoring the aunty makes her join your party anyway.

## Bugs
- **Health Insurance has no icon.** Steps: new run, Baba pick, open JhatPat, claim the free Mystery Box. When it rolls Health Insurance, the reveal shows a blank white circle, and the shop list row for Health Insurance is also blank. Every other item has art.
- **Wording mismatch (minor):** the meter label says "pheeka" and the result line says "Pheeki chai". To a non-speaker these read as two different things.
- I didn't see a fight-won banner in any screenshot. The fight ended and Baba's countdown appeared within about 2-3 seconds. It may exist and be too brief, so check it.
