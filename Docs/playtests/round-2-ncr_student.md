# Round 2: ncr_student

**Persona:** 20-year-old Delhi NCR college student on a phone (450x800 viewport). Impatient, skips text, taps fast, gets every NCR joke, shares funny stuff on Instagram.
**Build:** KADAK Chai, "dev build Sun 4 Oct 01:58", served at `http://localhost:8765/`, tab `seed`.
**Settings:** Music OFF (already off from a saved setting, I checked in the gear). Sound FX was also OFF, so audio isn't judged here.
**Session:** about 10 minutes of wall-clock time and about 75 tool actions (over the 60 budget, because many taps needed a second try; see Bugs). Furthest: **finished Day 4, beat the Broker (first boss)**. I stopped because I was over budget, not because I wanted to quit.

## The KADAK meter, honestly

- **Attempts: 3. KADAK hits: 0 that I could confirm.** Fight 1 and Fight 2: I tapped, the meter closed, and by my next screenshot (about 3 seconds later) there was no result and no line in the phone feed saying what I brewed or what buff I got. Boss: it timed out with "Chai got cold."
- **How hard is the dark middle?** I couldn't aim it at all. My screenshot-then-tap loop has a delay of a few seconds, so for me it was a coin flip, and that is a tester limitation, not the game's fault. On a real phone, 6 seconds is plenty of time to *see* the glass. The dark zone is about 1/7 of the bar, so a fast-tapping kid like me would hit theek most of the time and KADAK sometimes. That feels fair.
- **Was it fun?** The *idea* is great: chai strength as a timing meter, with pheeka/theek/KADAK labels, is instantly readable to any Indian kid and is my favourite new thing in this build. But the payoff is invisible. When it closes, I don't know whether I nailed it or what it did to the fight. Make the result loud and sticky: a big "KADAK!" stamp that stays for a beat, a buff chip on Bablu ("KADAK +x% ATK"), and a feed line ("Brewed: theek, +10% ATK"). Without that, it's a speed bump rather than a reward.
- "Chai got cold." is a funny fail line. Keep it.

## Top 3 frictions

1. **KADAK meter result is invisible.** After the tap, nothing tells me what I brewed or what it gave me. No lingering stamp, no buff chip, no feed line. It's the new mechanic and its payoff doesn't land.
2. **Baba feels rigged.** 2 of 3 picks fizzled ("No refunds") and the 4th visit repeated Day 1's exact cards. The fake-blessing joke is great once; twice in a row makes the choice feel pointless. Cap it at one fizzle per run early on, or show odds ("Baba's confidence: 70%").
3. **Taps sometimes didn't register on the first try** (gear, Close, Skip >>, Baba cards, JhatPat). It cost me a Baba blessing to the timer. This is very likely an artifact of the test harness's synthetic clicks on a WebGL canvas, but **Skip >> on the Broker outro ignored two taps in a row** while the text box worked, so it's worth one check on a real phone. Fights also feel slow even at 2x; a 3x or "skip to result" option would suit impatient players. (The pane may have been throttling, so treat that timing loosely.)

## Bugs / issues (with steps)

1. **Some taps ignored on first try (probably the harness).** Steps: on the title, tap the gear once and nothing happens; tap again and it opens. The same happened on Close, Skip >>, Meet the Astrologer, Baba cards and the JhatPat button. It looks like the click is processed at the previous pointer position. Most likely synthetic mouse events in the test browser, but please verify **Skip >> during the Broker's post-fight dialogue** on a real device (it ignored 2 consecutive taps).
2. **KADAK meter outcome not recorded anywhere.** Steps: Start Day 1 → the meter appears → tap mid-sweep → the meter closes and the fight starts with no visible result text that persists and no Messages entry. (A shout may flash too quickly to see; it was gone within about 3 seconds.)
3. **Post-boss Baba repeated Day 1's exact three cards** (Haggling / Pressure Horn / Wrong-Side Shortcut). Could be RNG, but it felt like a reroll bug.
4. Minor: on the boss day, my "Face the Boss" tap registered late, the meter ran while I thought I was still on the button screen, and I got "Chai got cold." without a fair shot. Probably related to item 1.
